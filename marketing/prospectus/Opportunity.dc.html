01 · The Opportunity
Canadian students deserve a national math tournament of their own.
While students south of the border have AMC, HMMT, and a dozen other storied competitions to aim for, Canada has had far fewer. The Canadian Math League is changing that: a free, team-based tournament that takes high school teams from an online Group Stage, through in-person Regional Championships, to a National final, built in partnership with the Canadian Mathematical Society.
CML is entering its first season, and has not yet announced a sponsor. We're looking for organizations who want to be part of that founding cohort, visible from day one, in front of an audience of Canada's most engaged young mathematicians, their teachers, and their parents.
A national audience
Reach high-achieving students, teachers, and parents across the country, online and in person.
Zero cost to enter
Every school competes for free. Your support removes the one barrier standing between a team and the bracket.
Founding sponsor status
Be the first name associated with CML, with the visibility and story that comes with getting in early.
Invest in Canadian STEM
Support the pipeline of Canadian students who go on to study and work in math, engineering, and computer science.
