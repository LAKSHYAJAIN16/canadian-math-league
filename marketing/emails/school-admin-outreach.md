# Email — School / administrator outreach

Use this to introduce CML to a principal, vice-principal, or department head who can help a math teacher get approval or funding to participate.

---

**Subject:** A free national math tournament for [School Name]

Dear [Administrator's Name],

I'm writing to introduce the Canadian Math League (CML), a new national math tournament for Canadian high schools, run in partnership with the Canadian Mathematical Society. I wanted to make sure [School Name] knows about it, since it's completely free to enter and only takes one teacher a few minutes to register a team.

A quick overview:

- **Free to enter** — no registration fee, no cost to the school or students
- **Team-based** — up to 3 teams of 4–6 students, so it's accessible beyond just your top one or two competitors
- **Three-stage tournament** — an online Group Stage (December 17, 2025), in-person Regionals (January 18–19, 2026, in Toronto or Vancouver), and a National final (February 21, 2026, Toronto)
- **Real prizes** — cash prizes for top teams at Nationals ($5,000 Champion, $2,500 Runner-Up, $1,500 Top Junior), plus medals and certificates for every finalist

We built CML because Canadian students have had far fewer national-level math competitions to aim for than their counterparts in the U.S. Participating gives students a concrete goal to work toward, and gives your school a low-effort way to support a strong extracurricular math program.

If you have a teacher who runs a math club or coaches competition math, please forward this along — registration is at [canadianmathleague.ca/register](https://canadianmathleague.ca/register). I'm also happy to answer any questions directly.
