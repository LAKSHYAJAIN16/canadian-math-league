# Email — Teacher outreach

Use this to invite a math teacher or club advisor to register their school for the Canadian Math League.

---

**Subject:** Register your students for Canada's math tournament, free

Hi [Teacher's Name],

I'm reaching out from the Canadian Math League (CML), a new, free, team-based math tournament for Canadian high schools, run in partnership with the Canadian Mathematical Society.

CML gives your students a shot at something most Canadian schools haven't had: a national tournament of their own, built around team-based rounds instead of one long solo exam. The season runs in three stages:

- **Group Stage** (online, December 17, 2025) — every registered team competes for free
- **Regionals** (in person, January 18–19, 2026, Toronto & Vancouver) — top teams advance
- **Nationals** (in person, February 21, 2026, Toronto) — six finalist teams compete for the national title and cash prizes

At the Group Stage, teams tackle four distinct formats: an AMC-style individual round (Canadian Open), a collaborative round solved together (Team Rush), a speed-and-accuracy problem grid (Capture the Problem), and fast head-to-head matchups.

Registration is free and takes a few minutes — you can register up to 3 teams of 4–6 students. Sample problem sets are available if you'd like to see the level before signing up.

Register here: [canadianmathleague.ca/register](https://canadianmathleague.ca/register)

Happy to answer any questions.
