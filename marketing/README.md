## Outreach emails

Copy-paste email templates in `emails/`:

- `teacher-outreach.md` — inviting a math teacher/club advisor to register, plus a
  pre-deadline reminder
- `school-admin-outreach.md` — introducing CML to a principal/administrator

All content (dates, prize amounts, team counts) is pulled from `lib/content/season.ts`
and the live site — double-check both stay in sync if those numbers change.
